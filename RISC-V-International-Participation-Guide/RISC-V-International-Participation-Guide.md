RISC-V International Participation Guide
1. Community Communication Platform: groups.io
RISC-V International uses the mailing list management system provided by groups.io as its community communication platform. RVI adopts the approach of building the groups.io community platform on its own server resources.

Portal: Refers to RVI's groups.io platform.

Mailing list: Can be understood as a distribution list. When a sender deems it necessary, they can send emails to a specific mailing list for everyone to participate in discussions. For new users, the act of adding themselves to this mailing list is called "subscribing" to a mailing list.

2. Becoming a RISC-V International Member
There are two main types of membership: Organization Member and Individual Member.

How to register your organization as an Organization Member:

Go to the official RVI registration page (https://riscv.org/members/join/) and click "JOIN AS ORGANIZATION".

Fill out the registration form and wait for RVI staff to contact the person listed in the form via email.

Further confirm relevant information via email and sign the RVI Membership Agreement sent by email.

After signing, notify RVI via email of the email addresses of the relevant personnel to be registered, and upload the LOGO.

3. User Operations
After creating an account on the platform, you can complete your profile, join groups, and join mailing lists of specific groups.

Joining Groups and Subscribing to Mailing Lists:

In the "Your Groups" dropdown at the top of the platform, you can see the groups you have joined. For example, RISC-V Main Group, allmem, Security HC, sig-trusted-computing, tech-ap-tee, tech-smmtt, etc. These are all Subgroups.

Joining a Subgroup means subscribing to the corresponding mailing list, and group emails will be automatically sent to your mailbox.

To join a group, take Security HC as an example:

Open the corresponding page (e.g., https://lists.riscv.org/g/security).
Click the "Join This Group" button.
After joining, you can modify the email push settings for this group via "Subscription -> Settings".
4. Joining Committees of Specific Groups and Obtaining Voting Rights
The above operations only involve joining mailing lists on the community platform and receiving information updates. These groups are actually various committees within RVI. For example, Security HC is a governing committee, and the SIGs/TGs under it are technical committees. To join these committees as a formal Participant and obtain voting rights, you need to communicate with the committee chair.

Taking joining Security HC as an example:

Confirm that you have obtained RISC-V International Membership.

Write an email to Security HC Chair Andrew Dellow (adellow@qti.qualcomm.com) expressing your intention to join Security HC as a Participant.

Voting rights can be applied for from Andrew Dellow after having a record of attending three consecutive Security HC regular meetings. In addition, you need to state that you can vote on behalf of your organization (not required if you are an Individual Member).

Notes:

To maintain voting rights, you must not miss more than two of any three consecutive regular meetings, and you must not miss all votes after your voting rights take effect.

For any vote, you can only participate if you obtained voting rights before the vote began.

Groups under Security HC, such as Trusted Computing SIG and SmMTT TG, also have their own independent committee management. You need to contact the respective chairs to join. The joining process, methods for obtaining voting rights, and related requirements are the same as above.
